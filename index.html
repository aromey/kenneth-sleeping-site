The problem

Most sleep advice treats the symptom.
Not the schedule.

01

You've tried the blackout curtains, the magnesium, the "no screens" rule — and you're still wide awake at 1am.

02

Generic sleep hygiene tips assume everyone's problem is the same. Yours has a specific cause — usually more than one.

03

What actually works is a plan built around your body's own rhythm, tested and adjusted week over week.

The process

Four steps. One rebuilt sleep system.

01

Assessment

A deep-dive intake covering your schedule, environment, habits, and history — plus two weeks of sleep tracking to see the real pattern, not the remembered one.

02

Custom plan

Kenny builds a plan specific to what your data shows: timing shifts, wind-down structure, environment changes, and the habits worth actually keeping.

03

Weekly check-ins

Short sessions to review what's working, troubleshoot what isn't, and adjust the plan as your sleep responds — nothing set-and-forget.

04

Sleep, on its own

By the end, the system runs without coaching. You know your own rhythm well enough to keep it — and fix it if life knocks it off track.
